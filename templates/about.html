About Our Project

Where Healthcare meets Artificial Intelligence

🌍 Our Vision

We envision a world where healthcare information is accessible to everyone. Early awareness can save lives, and we aim to empower users with smart tools.

👨‍💻 Who We Are

We are a team of developers, data science enthusiasts, and problem solvers passionate about using technology to improve healthcare accessibility.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to build an AI-powered platform that helps users identify possible diseases based on symptoms and provides guidance for better health decisions.

⚙️ How It Works

Our system uses machine learning models trained on medical datasets. By entering symptoms, users receive predictions along with precautions, diet suggestions, and lifestyle recommendations.
